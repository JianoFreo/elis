# User Manual

Steps below follow the approved use cases from Phase 3. Button and screen names may differ slightly in the live app.

## Roles
| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| Business Owner | Reviews branch data and discrepancies, approves adjustments, signs off the week, manages accounts and thresholds |
| Sales Staff | Records sales, counts stock, confirms deliveries, submits daily closing |
| Production Head | Records production output, creates dispatches, registers supplies and stock-in |
| Production Staff | Logs opening and closing inventory for each shift |

## Sales Staff

### Log in and select branch / shift
1. Open the system and enter your username and password.
2. Select your branch and shift.
3. Confirm to open your dashboard.

### Record a sale
1. Choose **Record Sale**.
2. Select the product and enter the quantity.
3. Save. The system computes the line total and updates stock.
4. If there is no internet, the sale is queued on your device and synced automatically when you are back online.

### Review the daily summary
1. Open the daily sales and stock summary.
2. Check totals against what you recorded.

### Count stock and submit daily closing
1. Count the stock on hand.
2. Enter the counted quantities.
3. If the count does not match the expected stock, the system flags a possible discrepancy.
4. Confirm and submit the daily closing.

### Confirm a delivery
1. Open the incoming dispatch.
2. Enter the quantities actually received.
3. Confirm receipt. The branch ledger updates.
4. If the quantities differ, flag a delivery discrepancy.

### View a discrepancy notice
Open your discrepancy notices to see what was flagged and its status.

## Production Head

### Record production output
Enter what was produced for the day. The ledger updates.

### Create a dispatch record
1. Choose **Create Dispatch Record**.
2. Select the products, quantities and batch number.
3. Save. Main hub stock is deducted automatically.

### Register supply items and thresholds
Add a supply item with its unit and low-stock threshold.

### Record supply stock-in
Enter the supply received, with supplier and cost.

### Monitor low-stock alerts
Open low-stock alerts and reports to see items below their threshold.

## Production Staff

### Log shift opening and closing inventory
1. At shift start, enter the opening quantities.
2. At shift end, enter the closing quantities.
3. The system computes the day's usage and triggers a low-stock alert when needed.

## Business Owner

### Review consolidated branch data
Open the consolidated view to see sales, exceptions and discrepancy flags across branches.

### Review dispatch status and alerts
Check dispatches against what branches confirmed, and read any alerts.

### Review and approve discrepancies
1. Open a flagged discrepancy.
2. Review expected quantity, counted quantity and variance.
3. Approve a stock adjustment if justified.

### Weekly audit
1. Include confirmed records in the weekly audit.
2. Review the weekly audit report.
3. Sign off and close the week.

### Manage accounts
Create staff accounts, configure their permissions, and delete accounts that are no longer needed.
